## Question

After the null-calibrated `t0 -> t1` distribution shifts, does a fresh independently sampled current checkpoint `t2` reproduce the `t1` basin?

The intended pattern for a persistent basin transition is:

`distance(t0,t1) large`, `distance(t0,t2) large`, while `distance(t1,t2)` is compatible with the permutation null.

- no adaptive expansion;
