## 4. Mathematical decision relevance

For a representation hypothesis H, the strict calibrated criterion can be expressed through downstream action dependence:

`DR(H) = I(A; H | E, K)`.

If `P(A|H,E,K) = P(A|not H,E,K)`, then `DR(H)=0` even when H itself is highly uncertain.

When only uncalibrated operational proxies exist, RAOS may use counterfactual distribution distance as a diagnostic, but not as Bayesian mutual information or production authority.
